About Mary‑Pauline

Passionate about sustainable livestock

Our story

Mary‑Pauline Allied Enterprise began with a vision: to provide healthy, ethically raised livestock to Lagos families and businesses. What started as a small poultry farm has grown into a diversified animal production hub, specialising in CatFish and chicken.

We combine modern farming techniques with traditional care, ensuring every animal is raised in a stress‑free environment. Our farm is located in Ojo, Lagos, with a commitment to traceability and quality.

Mrs. MaryPauline Ihejirika — CEO & founder, with over 15 years of experience in agribusiness.

What we stand for

Animal welfare
